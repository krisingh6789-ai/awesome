# Research and editorial record — 19 September 2026

## What was actually consulted

Seven online publications support 59 new explanatory sections in ten chapters. These are original AI-written explanations and examples, with short Hindi recaps, not copied textbook pages. Source consultation is not independent expert review. Links support the identified new sections, not every earlier core lesson or advanced guide. The photographs remain the scope source, not the teaching source.

### NCERT · Rights in the Indian Constitution
- URL: https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/keps202.pdf
- Edition / retrieval context: Indian Constitution at Work, chapter 2, reprint 2026–27
- Use and limits: Rights, constitutional remedies, Directive Principles and the rights–duties relationship. Historical examples and simplified legal descriptions are not treated as current consolidated law.

### NCERT · Legislature
- URL: https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/keps205.pdf
- Edition / retrieval context: Indian Constitution at Work, chapter 5, reprint 2026–27
- Use and limits: Bicameralism, legislative process, executive accountability and committees. Exact procedural thresholds require current constitutional text and House rules.

### NCERT · Federalism
- URL: https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/keps207.pdf
- Edition / retrieval context: Indian Constitution at Work, chapter 7, reprint 2026–27
- Use and limits: Federal design, division of powers and demands for autonomy. Its historical Planning Commission discussion is not a description of today's institutions.

### RBI · Monetary Policy overview
- URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_Overview.aspx?fn=2752
- Edition / retrieval context: Live overview consulted 19 September 2026
- Use and limits: Policy objective, MPC, inflation-targeting framework, liquidity instruments and operating target. No current policy-rate quotation is embedded.

### Union Budget · Budget at a Glance 2026–27
- URL: https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Budget_at_Glance/budget_at_a_glance.pdf
- Edition / retrieval context: 2026–27 Budget Estimates; overview and opening receipts/expenditure table
- Use and limits: Deficit definitions, effective capital expenditure and the dated worked budget example. These are estimates, not final accounts. The URL may later serve a newer budget.

### WTO · Principles of the trading system
- URL: https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/whatis_e/tif_e/fact2_e.htm
- Edition / retrieval context: Understanding the WTO; consulted 19 September 2026
- Use and limits: MFN, national treatment, bindings, predictability, liberalisation and development flexibilities. Agreement-specific disputes need the actual legal texts.

### IMF · Gross Domestic Product: An Economy’s All
- URL: https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/fandd/issues/Series/Back-to-Basics/gross-domestic-product-GDP
- Edition / retrieval context: Tim Callen, Finance & Development, Back to Basics; consulted 19 September 2026
- Use and limits: GDP, value added, production/income/expenditure approaches and nominal versus real output. Used for concepts, not India's current base year or statistical methodology.

## Editorial cautions

- Consulted NCERT chapters: Rights chunks 0–4, Legislature 0–3, Federalism 0–2; RBI overview both chunks; Budget opening overview/table; WTO principles first two chunks; IMF GDP first chunk. Do not infer that every linked document was read in its entirety.
- The official 2024 Constitution PDF was also inspected, but the extraction exposed its contents rather than the operative Articles. It is not cited as proof of a completed provision-by-provision legal review.
- NCERT contains pedagogical simplifications and historical material. The notes do not repeat its historical Planning Commission description as present fact, old NHRC membership descriptions as current law, or shorthand descriptions of retrospective criminal laws as a ban on all retrospective legislation. More precise legal distinctions in these original notes still need review against current provisions and judgments.
- IMF material is used for basic concepts, not its historical references to national-accounting standards or an Indian GDP base year. The simplified value-added and nominal/real examples are original teaching examples.
- The RBI target-period statement and Budget Estimates are expressly dated. No live repo-rate claim is made. Budget figures use ₹ crore and distinguish actuals, revised estimates and budget estimates.
- WTO material supports principles, not a comprehensive account of every agreement, dispute or current negotiating position.
- Every photographed chapter remains available, but only the ten above currently have this new research layer. The remaining core notes, unexpanded subheadings, full Hindi translations, expert review and missing source practice/answer pages are still outstanding.
- Source URLs can change and live budget URLs can be replaced. Editions and the retrieval date are recorded; third-party PDFs and full scraped texts have not been redistributed.
